# What is Filler Episodes

**A filler episode is a standalone installment in a long-running anime series that is not adapted from the original manga or light novel source material and does not advance the main plot.**

These episodes are typically created by the anime studio's writing team to allow the original manga author time to produce more source material, preventing the anime from catching up to and overtaking the published story. As a result, filler episodes often feature self-contained stories, side adventures for the main characters, comedic side-stories, or flashbacks to previously seen events. They exist primarily to maintain a consistent weekly broadcast schedule without resorting to extended hiatuses.

Filler episodes are most commonly found in extremely long-running "shonen" series like *Naruto*, *Bleach*, and *One Piece*. For example, in *Naruto*, entire multi-episode "filler arcs" were inserted during the time-skip period, featuring missions and villains not present in the manga. These episodes can vary in quality; some are criticized for pacing issues or out-of-character moments, while others are beloved for their character development or unique comedy. Viewers can usually identify filler episodes by consulting online "filler guides," which list which episodes are canon (from the source) and which are not.

For anime viewers, understanding filler is crucial for efficient watching, especially with series boasting hundreds of episodes. Fans who wish to follow only the core story as intended by the original creator will often use filler lists to skip these episodes entirely. Conversely, some viewers enjoy filler as bonus content that offers a break from high-stakes plots and explores the series' world and characters in a lower-pressure context. For the studios and broadcasters, filler is a practical production solution that keeps a popular series on the air and its commercial momentum going.
