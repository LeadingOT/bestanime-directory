# What is MAL Score

**The MAL Score is the community-driven, weighted average rating for an anime or manga series on MyAnimeList (MAL), the world's largest online anime and manga database.**

This score is calculated from the individual ratings (typically on a 1-10 scale) submitted by MAL's millions of registered users. It is not a simple average. The site employs a weighted ranking formula that considers the number of users who have rated a title and the distribution of those ratings to mitigate vote manipulation and ensure the score reflects a broader consensus. The result is a single, prominent number displayed on each title's page, serving as a quick, at-a-glance indicator of its general reception within the active anime community.

The MAL Score matters because it functions as a primary discovery and filtering tool for fans. On a platform like bestanime.info, which catalogs tools, understanding this metric helps users leverage MAL's data effectively. For example, a viewer might use a third-party tool to sort seasonal anime by their MAL Score to identify the most popular and critically acclaimed shows to watch. Similarly, someone browsing for classic series might filter a database to only show titles with a score above 8.0. It provides a data-backed starting point for deciding what to watch next, though it should be considered alongside personal tastes and written reviews.

However, it's important to interpret the score with context. Highly niche titles may have excellent but fewer ratings, affecting their weighted rank, while mainstream shows can sometimes be subject to "review bombing." The score represents the median opinion of a specific, engaged online community, not an absolute critical verdict.

**Related Terms:** **MyAnimeList (MAL):** The source platform hosting the scores. **AniList Score:** A similar community rating on a competing database platform. **Bayesian Estimate:** A type of weighted average similar to what MAL uses; used by other sites like IMDb. **Seasonal Chart:** A list of anime airing in a given season, often sortable by MAL Score.
